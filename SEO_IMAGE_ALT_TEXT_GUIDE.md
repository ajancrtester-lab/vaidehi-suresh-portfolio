## 🔍 Best Practices Followed:

### ✅ **Descriptive & Specific:**
- Every alt text describes what's in the image
- Includes performer name, location, art form
- Context-appropriate keywords

### ✅ **Natural Language:**
- Not keyword stuffing
- Reads naturally to humans
- Grammatically correct

### ✅ **Length Optimized:**
- 10-15 words per alt text
- Concise but informative
- Not too short, not too long

### ✅ **Consistent Format:**
```
[Title/Subject] - [Location] [Art Form] [Context] by [Artist Name]
```
